# Community Platform Concept

## PRODUCT CONCEPT

**Groups • Collaboration • Moderation • Communication • Reminders**

---

## 1. Core Ideology

### Alliances / Clans / Groups

Communities are organized into distinct groups, each with its own identity, members and activities.

### Discoverability

Groups are searchable by name and topic/subject, including subject-focused communities such as UGCET, GATE or JEE.

### Flexible Access

A group can be invite-only or open for anyone to join, depending on the creator's chosen permission model.

---

## 2. Group Lifecycle

### Creation

Create a group with a name, topic/subject and optional expiry date.

### Links

Generate a shareable/creatable link for convenient distribution.

### Joining

Users can search for groups and join when the access policy permits.

### Moderation

Groups can have moderators who manage day-to-day activity.

---

## 3. Moderation & Reporting

### Founder / Initial Moderator

The initial founder becomes the first moderator.

### Reporting

Members can report inappropriate content or behaviour for moderator review.

### Escalation

The concept specifies that 5 reports can trigger flagging to an upper moderation level.

---

## 4. Communication & Collaboration

### Room Reservation

Reserve a dedicated room for a scheduled community activity.

### Voice Chat / Messages

Provide voice-chat and messaging capabilities, similar in concept to a social audio space.

### PDF Sharing

Share study material, documents and reference PDFs.

### Text & Links

Share useful written content and external links directly inside a group.

---

## 5. Notifications & Engagement

### Notify Me

Users can subscribe to an activity, room or event for a future notification.

### Schedule & Reminder

Schedule sessions/tasks and send reminders before the planned time.

### Streaks & Alerts

Optional streaks support consistent participation, while alerts surface important or upcoming activities.

---

## 6. Conceptual User Flow

```text
Discover → Join → Participate → Schedule → Moderate
```

### Discover

Search by name / topic.

### Join

Open or invite-only.

### Participate

Chat / voice / files / links.

### Schedule

Reserve rooms & set reminders.

### Moderate

Report → review → escalate.

---

## 7. Feature Summary

| Area | Proposed capability |
|---|---|
| Community | Alliances / clans / groups |
| Discovery | Name + topic/subject search |
| Access | Invite-only or open joining |
| Moderation | Founder + moderators + reporting |
| Communication | Rooms, voice chat, messages |
| Sharing | PDFs, text and links |
| Productivity | Notify me, scheduling, reminders |
| Engagement | Streaks and alerts |
